Built a generative pre-trained transformer (GPT) from scratch — attention
blocks, tokenizer, and training loop — and trained it on a Shakespearean
text dataset to generate new text in the style of Shakespeare. One of my
first deep dives into how transformer language models actually work under
the hood, well before "build your own GPT" was a common exercise.
